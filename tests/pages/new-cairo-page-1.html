How to Buy an Apartment in the Fifth Settlement?

To be able to acquire an apartment within the Fifth Settlement area, it is necessary to follow a set of important procedures. Those intending to buy must first directly inspect the apartment in question, taking care to check its area and compare it with the provided floor plans.

It is also important to ensure that the building has obtained all necessary licenses from the relevant authorities and to review the permitted number of floors.

Second, the buyer is advised to request that the seller provide the chain of contracts for the property, which allows the buyer to understand the ownership record and how it has been transferred over time, starting from the first owner and up to the current status of the apartment.

Third, it may be necessary to obtain a building permit for the residential unit to be purchased. Therefore, the buyer must verify the number and date of the permit provided by the seller to ensure the legality of the property.

It is also essential to verify the identity of the current property owner and communicate with the relevant administrative authorities to check the owner's record and investigate any legal obstacles that may hinder the purchase process.

Personally inspecting the property and reviewing all documents related to it is a crucial step that precedes making the final decision to buy an apartment in the Fifth Settlement. These procedures ensure the legitimacy of the process and the validity of the documents provided, allowing for a smooth and trouble-free buying experience.
